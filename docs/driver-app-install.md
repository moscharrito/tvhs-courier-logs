# Getting the courier app on your phone

**Izy Global Services — University Health contract**

You do not need the App Store or Google Play. The app runs in your phone's
browser and you add it to your home screen, where it opens full screen and
behaves like any other app.

Takes about two minutes. Do it once, before your first shift.

---

## iPhone

1. Open **Safari**. It has to be Safari — Chrome on an iPhone cannot add an
   app to the home screen.
2. Go to **logs.izyglobalservices.com**
3. Sign in with the username and password you were given.
4. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow coming out of the top,
   at the bottom of the screen.
5. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
6. The name will say **TAG**. Tap **Add**, top right.

The icon is now on your home screen. Open it from there from now on, not from
Safari: opened that way it fills the screen and keeps you signed in.

## Android

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Go to **logs.izyglobalservices.com**
3. Sign in with the username and password you were given.
4. A bar may appear at the bottom offering to **install** the app. Tap it.
   If it does not, tap the **⋮** menu, top right, then **Add to Home screen**
   or **Install app**.
5. Tap **Install**.

If you were sent an installer file (`.apk`) instead, use that — it is the same
app. Your phone will warn you that it came from outside the Play Store; that is
expected, choose to continue.

---

## The first time you use it

**Allow location when asked.** The app asks once, the first time you record a
collection or a delivery. It attaches where you were to that delivery, which is
how the office can prove a package reached the right address if a patient ever
says it did not. It does **not** follow you around between stops, and it never
records anything when you are off shift.

**Go on shift when you start.** The button is at the top of your run. Until you
do, the office cannot give you any work — you will not appear on their board at
all.

**Go off shift when you finish.** If you are still carrying anything the screen
will say so. Those packages go back to the pharmacy they came from the same
day, before you go home.

---

## While you are working

- **The current stop is at the top**, large. Work down the list in order.
- **Directions** opens your normal maps app.
- **Dispatch** calls the office.
- **No signal?** Keep working. Everything you record is held on the phone and
  sent the moment you have a bar again, in the order it happened. Do not redo
  anything — you will not lose it.
- **Every delivery is handed to a person and signed for.** Nothing is left at a
  door. If nobody is there, record the attempt with a reason.

## If something goes wrong

- **Signed out unexpectedly?** Sign in again. A session lasts a full shift, so
  this should be rare.
- **The screen will not load?** Check you have signal, then close it and open
  it again from the home screen icon.
- **Forgotten your password?** Ring the office. Do not share a login with
  another driver — every delivery is recorded against whoever is signed in, and
  that record is what protects you in a dispute.

Ring dispatch for anything else. The number is on your run screen.
