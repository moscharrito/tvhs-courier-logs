Izy Collection Manifest

One sheet per pharmacy per pickup window — so Discharge needs two a day, 11:00 and 13:00. Signed once by the site lead who collects everything, instead of once per delivery.

  • It does not re-list the deliveries. The pharmacy's own list already enumerates them, and handwriting 241 Rx references would be worse than 241 signatures. This signs against that list and records the counts.
  • The counted figure is written by the lead, not copied. Two numbers that must agree, written by two people, is the whole control. A pre-filled count is a count nobody checked.
  • Refrigerated is counted separately, because the cooler is the thing that gets missed and warm arrivals are a live complaint about the incumbent.

Prints on US Letter. Reads correctly in black and white.
