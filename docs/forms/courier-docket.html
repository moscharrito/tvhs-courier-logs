Izy Delivery Docket

A blank pad. One sheet per delivery, filled in by hand, cut along the scissor lines in order:

  • For pharmacy staff — stays at the counter when the courier collects.
  • For driver — stays in the van and carries the handover record.
  • For patient — left with the medication, or brought back and signed off by the pharmacy if the delivery failed.

The Rx reference is written on all three parts. With no docket number printed, that reference is what matches the pharmacy's copy to the patient's once the sheet is torn apart, so it needs to go on every part even when the rest is left blank.

Prints on US Letter. Reads correctly in black and white, so it runs on a mono laser or as a carbonless pad.
