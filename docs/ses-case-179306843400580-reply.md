## Why this is urgent

Until production access is granted SES will only deliver to verified
identities, which means **a driver or pharmacist who forgets their password
cannot receive a code**. The application side works end to end — that was
proved by watching `/health` move to `400 MessageRejected` the moment a code
was requested for an unverified address — so the sandbox is the only thing
in the way.

## The thing to do before access lands, not after

Notifications addressed to `@example.invalid` from rehearsal runs are still
queued. The moment production access is granted those become real delivery
attempts and every one hard-bounces. A bounce rate that high on a new
production account risks immediate suspension, which is a worse position
than the sandbox.

Clear them first — dry run, then apply:

    node server/scripts/clear-mail-queue.mjs --base https://logs.izyglobalservices.com --i-mean-production --domain example.invalid
    ... --apply
