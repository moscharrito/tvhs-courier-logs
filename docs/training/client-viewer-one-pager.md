# Deliveries: your view

For pharmacy staff at University Health. One page.

You have a sign-in to see what happened to the deliveries **your pharmacy**
sent out. No phone call, no waiting for a report.

---

## Signing in

Your address and a password, at the address Izy gave you. If you forget it,
ask your Izy contact to reset it; they cannot read it.

You will see only your own pharmacy, or pharmacies, named at the top of the
screen beside the date.

---

## Today, in four numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| **sent to us** | Everything you gave us for this day |
| **still out** | With a courier now |
| **delivered** | Done, signed for |
| **not delivered** | Came back. Shown in red, because these are the only ones needing you |

Pick any date with **From**. **Today** returns to today.

---

## The list

**Failures and anything overdue are at the top**, because they are the rows
that need a person.

Each row shows **Your reference** (the number on your own list, so you can
find it in your system), the patient, the status and the times.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Received by us | We have it on the list; no courier yet |
| Waiting for a courier | Ready to go out |
| Courier assigned | On a run, not collected yet |
| On the way | The courier has it |
| Delivered | Handed over and signed for |
| Not delivered | Came back. The reason is on the row |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn before it went out |

Search by **Your reference** (for example `RX-1234`) to find one delivery.

---

## Opening one delivery

Tap a row for **What was sent**, **Times**, **What happened**, **Signed for
by**, and the **Address** it went to.

**Proof of delivery** is a one-page PDF: the date and time, where it was
collected, where it went, what was in it, and the signature of the person who
received it. Download it for your own records at any time.

Where a delivery was left without a signature because the medication allowed
it, the document says **Left without a signature** and why, rather than
leaving a blank that might look like a printing fault.

---

## What you will not see, and why

**Courier first names only.** You see *"Ana"*, not a full name. Our couriers
are our staff, and their surnames are not part of your record.

**No live map.** You see where a delivery is in its journey, not where a
person is.

**Only your own pharmacy.** If your pharmacy has not been set up yet, you will
correctly see nothing at all rather than somebody else's patients.

**No prices.** The rate schedule is between Izy and University Health
procurement. Invoices go to your finance team.

---

## If something is wrong

**A delivery failed.** The reason is on the row. Usually it is something only
you can fix: a wrong address, a patient who has moved, a shipment that was
incomplete. The medication has already been brought back to a pharmacy the
same day.

**A delivery is late.** Overdue rows sort to the top. Telephone dispatch if it
matters now; the screen will not make it arrive faster.

**Something looks wrong in the record.** Tell your Izy contact. Records are
never quietly edited: a correction is made as a correction, so it stays
visible that something changed and who changed it.

**You cannot sign in.** Too many wrong passwords locks the account for fifteen
minutes; it clears itself. Anything else, ask your Izy contact.

---

## The one thing worth knowing about the record

Every delivery carries a chain of custody: who collected it, when, who
received it, and their signature, recorded at the time by the courier
standing there. It cannot be edited afterwards. That is the point, and it is
what the proof of delivery document prints.
